TL;DR: Day1T1 does not collect your data nor send it to any server. Everything stays on your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Day1T1, a completely free app to help Type 1 diabetes patients manage their daily condition. Built by Omar abd el-gbbar — himself a diabetic patient — to serve Arabic-speaking patients.

We take your privacy seriously. This policy clearly explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

2. What Data Does the App Collect?

The app stores the following data on your device only:

  • Personal info: Name, date of birth, gender, height, weight
  • Medical data: Blood glucose readings, insulin doses, meals and carbs, hypo events, exercise, reminders
  • Optional data: City, occupation, diagnosis date, glucose meter type

3. Where is Your Data Stored?

On your device only — nothing is sent to any server.

All data is stored in a local SQLite database within your device's storage. The app does not require an internet connection to function, nor does it transmit your data to any external server.

4. Do You Share My Data with Third Parties?

No. We do not share your data with any third party because we don't possess it — it lives on your device.

The only exceptions:

  • You yourself: When you use the "Share Doctor Report" feature, YOU choose to share a PDF or text file with a specific contact (WhatsApp, email, etc).
  • Backup: When you use "Export Backup," a JSON file is created on your device, and you decide where to save it.

5. App Permissions

  • Notifications: For meal reminders, medical reminders, and post-meal/correction follow-up alerts.
  • Exact alarms: To ensure scheduled reminders arrive precisely on time.
  • Vibration: To alert you for critical notifications (e.g., post-hypo recheck).
  • Battery optimization exemption (optional): Helps reminders arrive on time on devices that aggressively kill apps (Honor / Xiaomi / Huawei).

6. Your Rights Over Your Data

Your data is fully yours. At any time, you can:

  • View: All your data is visible inside the app.
  • Edit: You can edit any saved reading, meal, or event.
  • Delete: You can delete items individually or all data at once from app settings.
  • Export: "Backup" feature exports all your data as a JSON file.
  • Wipe: Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data with it.

7. Children and Minors

The app is designed for diabetes patients of all ages, including children under parental supervision. We do not request or collect any age-related data beyond date of birth (which stays on the device).

If the user is a minor, we strongly recommend they use the app under parental supervision, especially for dose calculations.

8. Security

Your data is stored in Android's sandboxed app storage. Other apps on your device cannot read your data.

Security recommendation: Enable a strong screen lock on your device (PIN/pattern/biometric) to protect your data.

9. Crashlytics Tool (Future Versions)

In future versions, we may add Firebase Crashlytics by Google for crash reporting. This tool:

  • Sends only technical data (device model, Android version, error message, code line where the crash occurred)
  • Does not send any health or personal data
  • Helps us fix bugs quickly

When this tool is enabled, we will update this privacy policy to inform you.

10. Changes to This Policy

When this policy is updated, the "Last Updated" date at the top will be revised. Material changes will be announced inside the app.

11. Medical Disclaimer

Important Notice: Day1T1 is only an assistance tool — not a substitute for medical advice. Do not rely on dose calculations in the app without consulting your healthcare provider. In emergencies, contact your doctor or emergency services immediately.
